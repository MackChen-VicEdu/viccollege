Why Choose a Career as a Personal Support Worker?

Personal Support Workers (PSWs) are among the most essential and respected healthcare professionals in Canada. With Ontario's rapidly aging population and growing healthcare infrastructure, certified PSWs enjoy unmatched job security, flexible shifts, and meaningful daily patient impact.

Abundant Job Opportunities

PSWs are in extreme high demand across Ontario hospitals, long-term care homes (LTC), retirement residences, and home healthcare agencies, ensuring rapid job placement upon graduation.

Personal Fulfillment

Make a genuine difference every day by providing compassionate, dignified physical and emotional care that directly enhances the independence and quality of life for seniors and patients.

Competitive Compensation

Earn competitive hourly wages ($20–$28/hr) with opportunities for shift premiums, overtime, union benefits, comprehensive dental/medical plans, and paid vacation time.

Flexible Hybrid Learning

Study live interactive theory online from home, paired with hands-on practice in our fully equipped on-campus hospital simulation lab in Markham & North York.

Official NACC Curriculum Modules (15 Subjects)

Our comprehensive curriculum covers all 15 core vocational modules mandated by the National Association of Career Colleges (NACC) and Ontario Ministry guidelines:

  • 1 PSW Foundations: Role, responsibilities, scope of practice, legal boundaries & healthcare ethics.
  • 2 Safety and Mobility: Body mechanics, ergonomics, patient transfer techniques, infection control & WHMIS.
  • 3 Body Systems & Anatomy: Comprehensive overview of human anatomy, physiology, aging processes & vital signs.
  • 4 Assisting with Personal Hygiene: Bed baths, oral hygiene, skin integrity prevention, grooming & dignity care.
  • 5 Abuse and Neglect: Identification, institutional abuse reporting protocols & client rights advocacy.
  • 6 Household Management, Nutrition & Hydration: Meal planning, special dietary requirements, therapeutic diets & feeding assistance.
  • 7 Care Planning & Documentation: Restorative care goals, electronic health documentation (EHR) & reporting to RNs/RPNs.
  • 8 Assisting the Family / Growth & Development: Family dynamics, child development, supportive care across the lifespan.
  • 9 Assisting the Dying Person: Palliative care, end-of-life comfort, hospice support & bereavement protocols.
  • 10 Assisting with Medications: Pharmacological routes, medication reminders, blister packs & error reporting.
  • 11 Cognitive & Mental Health Issues: Alzheimer's disease, dementia care, depression, delirium & acquired brain injuries.
  • 12 Common Health Conditions: Diabetes, cardiovascular diseases, stroke, respiratory conditions, arthritis & cancer care.
  • 13 Gentle Persuasive Approaches (GPA): Evidence-based dementia de-escalation techniques & patient-centered behavioral care.
  • 14 Clinical Placement (Facility - 200+ Hours): Supervised on-site practicum in accredited Ontario Long-Term Care (LTC) nursing homes.
  • 15 Clinical Placement (Community - 110+ Hours): Hands-on in-home patient support with community healthcare agencies.

Admission Requirements

  • Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD / Grade 12) or Canadian / International equivalent evaluation.
  • Mature Student Status: 18 years of age or older with passing score on the Wonderlic Scholastic Level Exam (administered free on campus).
  • Clear Vulnerable Sector Police Check (VSS).
  • Standard Medical & Immunization Clearance Form (TB 2-step test, Hepatitis B, Influenza, COVID-19).
  • Proficiency in English communication.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get government funding (Better Jobs Ontario) to cover my tuition?
Yes! Eligible candidates (unemployed, laid-off, former EI recipients, self-employed, gig workers, or low-income PR/Citizens) can qualify for up to $28,000+ in non-repayable government grants covering 100% of tuition, books, transportation, and living allowance. Our advisors assist you throughout the entire application process free of charge.
How long does the PSW course take to complete?
The program takes between 23 to 30 weeks to complete, depending on whether you choose the accelerated daytime stream or flexible evening/weekend stream.
Do I need to find my own clinical placement?
No! Our dedicated clinical placement coordinators handle the entire placement process and assign you to one of our accredited partner long-term care facilities and community health organizations across the GTA.
What is the starting salary for PSWs in Ontario?
PSWs in Ontario typically earn between $20.00 to $28.00 per hour, with additional government hourly retention wage enhancements, overtime opportunities, and comprehensive employee benefits in unionized hospitals and nursing homes.
