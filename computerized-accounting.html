Comprehensive 30-Week Curriculum Modules (10 Core Subjects)

Our structured curriculum covers all essential competencies required by Canadian employers, from fundamental double-entry bookkeeping to advanced ERP systems and complex corporate taxation:

  • 1 Canadian Financial Accounting Principles: GAAP / ASPE standards, double-entry bookkeeping, general journals, ledgers, trial balance & financial statements.
  • 2 QuickBooks Desktop & Online (QBO): Business setup, chart of accounts, invoicing, bills, inventory, bank feeds & reconciliations.
  • 3 Sage 50 Accounting (Simply Accounting): Sub-ledgers, job costing, departmental accounting, multi-currency & bank reconciliations.
  • 4 Sage 300 / ACCPAC ERP Accounting: Enterprise ERP navigation, multi-entity consolidations, GL/AP/AR modules & month-end closing.
  • 5 Canadian Payroll Compliance & Administration: NPI standards, statutory deductions (CPP, EI, Tax, EHT, WSIB), ROE issuance & T4/T4A filing.
  • 6 Canadian Personal Taxation (T1): Personal income tax laws, deductions, credits, RRSP optimization & Profile/TaxPrep software filing.
  • 7 Canadian Corporate Taxation (T2) & GST/HST: Corporate tax returns, active business income, CCA schedules, GST/HST & CRA audits.
  • 8 Advanced Excel for Financial Modeling: XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, dynamic Pivot Tables, consolidations, variance models & dashboards.
  • 9 Full-Cycle Accounting Simulation: Practical cycle simulation for manufacturing, retail & services, adjusting entries & year-end working papers.
  • 10 Career Coaching & CPA Interview Prep: Canadian accounting resume workshops, CPA behavioral/technical interview drills & placement assistance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I study the Accounting, Tax and Payroll program online?
Yes! All theoretical lectures and software simulations are delivered live online with interactive CPA instructors. Students can participate from home across Ontario while accessing virtual labs and instructor office hours.
Do I need a prior background in math or accounting to enroll?
No prior accounting experience is required. The curriculum begins with fundamental bookkeeping and double-entry concepts before progressively advancing to corporate accounting software and Canadian taxation.
What accounting software will I learn in this program?
You will gain intensive hands-on experience in QuickBooks Desktop, QuickBooks Online (QBO), Sage 50 (Simply Accounting), Sage 300 (ACCPAC), Taxprep / Profile, and Advanced Excel for Finance.
Are there government grants available for this diploma program?
Yes! Victoria College's Accounting, Tax and Payroll program is fully approved for the Better Jobs Ontario (Second Career) grant program, providing up to $28,000+ in non-repayable government funding for eligible applicants.
